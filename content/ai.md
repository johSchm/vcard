lead: I work *with* AI, not around it.

I use AI every day to work faster and better. Three principles guide how.

## Communication first
icon: fas fa-users

The more routine work machines take over, the more the human part counts.

- Explain ideas clearly
- Present results so others can act on them
- Lead and connect people

## Agents are a team I lead
icon: fas fa-project-diagram

I work with AI agents the way I work with a team.

- Delegate tasks with clear context
- Agents research, code and report back
- I decide what is used

## Critical judgment
icon: fas fa-balance-scale

I learned my craft before AI, so I can check the results myself.

- Verify against primary sources
- Cross-check claims
- Reject hallucinations, learn from what holds
