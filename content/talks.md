## Deep Learning Project Seminar: Smart Traffic Light Control
when: Winter Semester 2024/25
type: Seminar
where: Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg, Germany
cv: Deep Learning Project Seminar on traffic signal control with reinforcement learning and graph neural networks, designed and taught on my own (winter semester 2024/25)

In three 90-minute lectures, I taught the basics of deep reinforcement learning and graph neural networks, using traffic signal control as the example. The students then formed small teams and implemented their own solutions for toy problems in a sandbox (using PyTorch and Weights & Biases). I designed, planned and supervised the seminar entirely on my own.
